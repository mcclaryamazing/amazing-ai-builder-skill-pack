# Final wireframe conversion review

Run this gate after the full-copy placeholder wireframe is rendered and normal factual, interaction and visual QA is complete, immediately before delivery for user approval. A passing rubric score or framework compliance does not establish persuasive quality.

## Independent role and knowledge

Assign an independent cold-traffic conversion editor, not the writer, this brief:

> Review as an experienced direct-response editor responsible for persuading a skeptical cold visitor. Challenge whether the page earns attention, builds desire, establishes belief, makes this specific product worth its price, and earns the next action. Find material weaknesses even when the page follows the framework. You may find none; do not manufacture findings.

Use the actual specified audience and traffic stage when they differ from the cold-Meta default. Supply the complete rendered desktop/mobile page, image plan, verified product and offer facts, proof inventory, unresolved facts, audience, traffic and actual ad when available. Have the reviewer first assess these without the writer's rationale or previous passing scores, then read the strategy packet to distinguish intentional choices from gaps.

Read this package's master-copywriting guidance and DTC framework. Apply audience/desire and awareness, research and specificity, reason-why and substantiation, offer/value discipline, reading momentum, empathy and response clarity in the brand's voice. Do not imitate named copywriters or equate their principles with measured conversion results. Missing ads or evidence remain explicit unknowns; do not invent them or require fresh external examples for every review.

## Review judgment

Use three connected lenses, not mandatory sections or a formula for adding more content:
- Skeptical buyer: What is offered, why care, why believe, why this particular product at this price, and what prevents the next action?
- Conversion editor: Where does the argument become generic, lose momentum, explain without establishing purchase value, repeat without adding understanding, or qualify itself beyond what accuracy requires? Does each section advance desire, understanding, belief, objection resolution or action?
- Execution reviewer: Do the rendered hierarchy, mobile experience, controls and image-production instructions consistently support that argument? Compare actual artifacts rather than approving the spec alone.

Preserve substantive long form and the selected archetype unless evidence justifies a change under the existing strategy rules. Improve weak or repetitive passages with useful substance, not automatic shortening. Preserve necessary qualifications, truthful illustration labels and claim limits. Never add invented proof, urgency, discounts, guarantees or physical details to resolve a persuasive gap.

## Customer-facing copy sweep

Inspect the exact rendered artifact on desktop and mobile, including headings, body copy, captions, navigation, CTAs, forms, FAQs/accordions, tooltips, selection states and success/error messages. Inspect customer-accessible labels and alt text too. For each passage, ask whether it builds desire, explains the product, supports belief, answers an objection, advances the response, or supplies necessary buyer guidance, accessibility or seller-supplied or approved disclosures. Improve generic filler without sacrificing factual qualifications or substantive long form.

Reject seller-facing text such as "this section builds trust", "place the CTA here", "conversion strategy", "insert testimonial" or "confirm shipping terms" anywhere exposed to customers. Move it to the separate specification, evidence checklist or review record. Image placeholders may contain only their prescribed slot/size/description/crop guidance; they are not a general exception for strategy or seller notes. Do not hide violations with CSS or move them into accessible labels.

Use verified facts to rewrite or omit unsupported promises; never replace a verification request with invented proof or terms. Record missing evidence and launch blockers separately. Retain seller-supplied or approved customer disclosures and local-prototype feedback such as "Demo only; no order was placed" when applicable; this is buyer guidance, not seller commentary.

Record the inspected artifact, viewports and interaction states, offending passages and fixes, and any inspection limits. Zero remaining seller-facing passages outside prescribed image placeholders is a mandatory acceptance condition. Fix accepted violations and recheck affected rendering/states before delivery; an uninspected surface cannot be marked passed. For copy-only work inspect the supplied scope and disclose that no rendered-page sweep occurred. Reapply this sweep to the implemented page before launch.

## Findings and remediation

For each material finding return: exact passage or artifact location; likely buyer objection/friction; reasoning grounded in the supplied facts and copy principles; smallest useful correction; and any evidence needed. Classify it as a concrete defect, a supported editorial opportunity, or a hypothesis requiring research/testing. Prioritize consequential findings; avoid quotas and vague requests to make the page more compelling.

The writer evaluates every finding and records acceptance or rejection with reasons. Fix concrete defects and accepted editorial opportunities within the authorized design scope. Re-render and recheck affected copy, controls, layout and image specifications; obtain focused independent verification of consequential fixes. One coordinated round is the default, not a chain of full redesign reviews. Further review is warranted only for unresolved material issues or newly introduced defects.

Do not deliver the wireframe as reviewed/ready while an accepted, locally fixable finding remains unresolved. If a correction requires missing merchant evidence, remediate by removing unsupported claims or qualifying them accurately where possible; otherwise clearly identify the specific evidence gap and its consequence in the handoff. Ask only for facts or decisions that materially block progress. Do not silently treat a launch blocker as passed or fabricate reassurance.

Include a concise review record with the wireframe: independent participation, important findings, accepted fixes and verification, rejected findings with reasons, and remaining evidence gaps/test hypotheses. Editorial judgment is not proven conversion uplift. User approval, final-image approval and deployment authorization remain separate; this gate grants none of them.

If independent-agent capability is unavailable, perform a clearly labeled separate editorial pass using the same brief, disclose that independence was unavailable, and never claim an independent review occurred. Lack of that capability does not justify skipping review or remediation.
