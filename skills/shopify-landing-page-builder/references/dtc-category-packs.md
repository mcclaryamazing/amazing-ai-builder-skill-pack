# DTC Category Packs

Use this reference when product category should shape frictions, proof, section sequence, offer mechanics, visual direction, or compliance caution.

## Apparel

Primary frictions:

- fit, size, feel, returns, quality, styling, color choice

Recommended hero:

- product/model gallery
- visible rating or placeholder proof
- size selector or fit guidance
- clear price
- `Select Size` CTA
- shipping/returns microcopy

Recommended sections:

- fit/feel value grid
- fabric or material section
- shop the look
- style or fit comparison
- reviews with fit metadata
- UGC/lifestyle gallery
- FAQ for sizing, returns, and care

Offer patterns:

- bundle discount, multi-buy pricing, free shipping threshold, first-order discount, fit guarantee

Avoid:

- `Add to Cart` before size selection
- no size guidance
- reviews without fit context
- low-quality product photography

## Food, Beverage, And Supplements

Primary frictions:

- taste, habit fit, ingredient trust, price per serving, subscription terms, health/claim credibility

Recommended hero:

- product packshot plus serving/use visual
- rating/review count or placeholder
- subscription savings if relevant
- free gift/starter kit if real
- `Choose Flavors`, `Choose a Pack`, or `Get Started` CTA

Recommended sections:

- ingredient transparency
- science/formulation explanation
- comparison vs alternatives
- value per serving
- how to use
- subscription benefits
- taste/results guarantee if real
- reviews/UGC
- FAQ for ingredients, allergens, cancellation, and shipping

Offer patterns:

- subscription discount, free starter kit, free shipping, money-back guarantee, bundle discount, first-order discount

Avoid:

- unverified health claims
- unclear subscription pricing
- generic healthy copy
- no taste reassurance

## Beauty And Personal Care

Primary frictions:

- will it work for me, shade/skin match, routine fit, safety, ingredients, results

Recommended hero:

- result-led lifestyle image plus product image
- rating/review count or placeholder
- shade/routine help when relevant
- `Add to Bag` or `Take the Quiz` CTA
- free shipping/returns microcopy

Recommended sections:

- benefits
- how to use
- ingredients
- compliant before/after only when substantiated
- verified clinical or user results
- UGC gallery
- routine cross-sells
- reviews
- FAQ for shade, skin type, and returns

Offer patterns:

- bundle sets, free shipping, routine discount, first-order discount, quiz/shade finder

Avoid:

- unsupported before/after claims
- unclear shade selection
- weak product education
- founder reliance without proof

## Home, Furniture, Cookware, And Decor

Primary frictions:

- price, fit/dimensions, shipping, returns, cleaning, durability, material safety, style compatibility

Recommended hero:

- product-on-white plus lifestyle context
- price/discount if verified
- configuration controls
- reviews or placeholder proof
- financing if high-AOV and real
- `Add to Cart` with price or selection CTA
- shipping/returns/guarantee line

Recommended sections:

- material/spec education
- dimensions
- lifestyle use cases
- comparison table
- care/cleaning
- warranty/returns
- UGC/reviews
- cross-sells/accessories
- FAQ

Offer patterns:

- bundle savings, seasonal sale, free shipping, financing, warranty, return guarantee

Avoid:

- no dimensions
- no shipping clarity
- weak return reassurance
- style-heavy page with no operational confidence

## Pet And Baby

Primary frictions:

- safety, effectiveness, convenience, subscription terms, fit/size/stage, trust, health claims

Recommended hero:

- warm lifestyle image
- clear product/offer
- expert/vet/parent proof if real
- rating/review count or placeholder
- `Get Started`, `Choose Size`, or `Find Recipe` CTA
- guarantee or free shipping if verified

Recommended sections:

- how it works
- safety/ingredient proof
- stage or size explanation
- comparison
- subscription or plan logic
- UGC/testimonials
- founder/expert proof when real
- FAQ
- guarantee

Offer patterns:

- free gift, first-order discount, subscription savings, free shipping, money-back guarantee, bundle supply

Avoid:

- unsupported medical/health claims
- unclear subscription future pricing
- cuteness without safety proof

## Wellness, Electronics, And Recovery Tech

Primary frictions:

- price, credibility, setup, daily usage, app/device value, warranty, financing, health claims

Recommended hero:

- premium lifestyle or device render
- specific outcome
- financing/trial if available
- proof cue
- `Shop Now`, `Try Free`, or accurate next-step CTA

Recommended sections:

- how it works
- science/results with substantiation
- use cases
- app/device ecosystem
- comparison table
- specs
- financing/trial/warranty
- reviews
- FAQ

Offer patterns:

- free trial, financing, warranty, HSA/FSA only if valid, free shipping, money-back guarantee

Avoid:

- technical jargon without benefit translation
- regulated claims without substantiation
- high price without financing or risk reversal

## Outdoor Gear And Utility Products

Primary frictions:

- durability, technical specs, price, use-case fit, warranty, shipping, compatibility

Recommended hero:

- product-on-white
- specific utility promise
- review proof or placeholder
- price
- `Add to Cart` or selection CTA
- trust strip

Recommended sections:

- feature tiles
- annotated product image
- in-use lifestyle section
- specs
- comparison
- FAQ
- reviews with use cases
- accessory cross-sells

Offer patterns:

- free shipping, warranty, bundle discounts, financing for high-AOV

Avoid:

- feature list without use-case explanation
- no specs
- no durability proof
- weak warranty visibility

## Custom, Print, And Configurable Goods

Primary frictions:

- quality uncertainty, customization effort, design confidence, shipping timing, proof of final result

Recommended hero:

- premium product photography
- customization CTA
- starting price if verified
- sample or design help offer when real
- trust cue

Recommended sections:

- sample pack bridge
- how customization works
- design options
- product tiers
- finish/material comparison
- customer examples
- FAQ
- guarantee

CTA examples:

- `Start Designing`
- `Customize`
- `Get a Sample`
- `Design It For Me`

Avoid:

- forcing customization before explaining quality
- no sample bridge
- no design support path
- unclear production/shipping timeline
