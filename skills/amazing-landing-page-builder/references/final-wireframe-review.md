# Final wireframe conversion review

Run this gate after the full-copy placeholder wireframe is rendered and normal factual, interaction and visual QA is complete, immediately before delivery for user approval. A passing rubric score or framework compliance does not establish persuasive quality.

## Independent role and knowledge

Assign an independent cold-traffic conversion editor, not the writer, this brief:

> Review as an experienced direct-response editor responsible for persuading a skeptical cold visitor. Challenge whether the page earns attention, builds desire, establishes belief, makes this specific product worth its price, and earns the next action. Find material weaknesses even when the page follows the framework. You may find none; do not manufacture findings.

Use the actual specified audience and traffic stage when they differ from the cold-Meta default. Supply the complete rendered desktop/mobile page, image plan, verified product and offer facts, proof inventory, unresolved facts, audience, traffic and actual ad when available. Have the reviewer first assess these without the writer's rationale or previous passing scores, then read the strategy packet to distinguish intentional choices from gaps.

Read this package's master-copywriting guidance and DTC framework. Apply audience/desire and awareness, research and specificity, reason-why and substantiation, offer/value discipline, reading momentum, empathy and response clarity in the brand's voice. Do not imitate named copywriters or equate their principles with measured conversion results. Missing ads or evidence remain explicit unknowns; do not invent them or require fresh external examples for every review.

## Review judgment

Use three connected lenses, not mandatory sections or a formula for adding more content:
- Skeptical buyer: What is offered, why care, why believe, why this particular product at this price, and what prevents the next action?
- Conversion editor: First assess persuasive completeness: does this page persuade someone who has not yet decided they need the product, or mostly help an already-convinced shopper choose a deal? Which important reasons are merely asserted rather than developed, and where would truthful explanation, detail or available evidence materially improve desire, understanding or belief? Then assess redundancy: what does each recurrence accomplish here, and what would the reader lose if it were removed? Preserve deliberate reinforcement, scanning support, recaps and decision-point information even without a new fact. Combine or delete only material without a useful role; do not automatically favor expansion or deletion. Could visual offer presentation clarify purchase details without replacing the product argument? Assess specificity, momentum and purchase value throughout.
- Execution reviewer: Do the rendered hierarchy, mobile experience, controls and image-production instructions consistently support that argument? Compare actual artifacts rather than approving the spec alone.

Preserve a substantive long-form argument and the selected archetype unless evidence justifies an overall short-form change under the existing strategy rules. Develop underexplained reasons using known facts and preserve purposeful reinforcement under `../SKILL.md`. Delete or combine sections with no useful local role, without performance evidence for each cut or replacement content to preserve length or a numbered count. Check the product-desire answer without the promotion and the stated basis for any promotion-led opening; a brief containing a discount is not sufficient. Preserve necessary qualifications, truthful illustration labels and material claim limits. Never add invented proof, urgency, discounts, guarantees or physical details to resolve a persuasive gap.

## Customer-facing copy sweep

Inspect the exact rendered artifact on desktop and mobile, including headings, body copy, captions, navigation, CTAs, forms, FAQs/accordions, tooltips, selection states and success/error messages. Inspect customer-accessible labels and alt text too. Check browsing before purchase actions separately from post-click feedback: would setup or preview commentary still belong if checkout were connected tomorrow? FAQs should answer product, offer or actual purchase-term questions, not temporary implementation limitations. Judge purpose and interaction state; "preview" or "demo" can be legitimate product language. For each passage, ask whether it builds desire, explains the product, supports belief, answers an objection, advances the response, or supplies necessary buyer guidance, accessibility or seller-supplied or approved disclosures. Assess both missing depth and unnecessary repetition; preserve material information, useful explanation and purposeful reinforcement. Do not classify a passage as filler solely because its idea appears elsewhere.

Reject seller-facing text such as "this section builds trust", "place the CTA here", "conversion strategy", "insert testimonial" or "confirm shipping terms" anywhere exposed to customers. Move it to the separate specification, evidence checklist or review record. Image placeholders may contain only their prescribed slot/size/description/crop guidance; they are not a general exception for strategy or seller notes. Do not hide violations with CSS or move them into accessible labels.

Use verified facts to rewrite or omit unsupported material promises; never replace a verification request with invented proof or terms. Check facts in the background and deliver finished persuasive customer copy. Record only unresolved facts that materially affect proposed published copy or an honest purchase decision in separate notes; omit nonessential evidence gaps. Retain seller-supplied or approved customer disclosures. For an unconnected purchase action, show truthful feedback such as "Demo only; no order was placed and no payment was taken" in its immediate interaction response; never imply checkout or an order succeeded. Unless a visibly labeled prototype was requested, this exception does not permit development-status notices in the page, footer, FAQ, offer microcopy or persistent banners. Put preview status, missing connections and installation instructions in the seller handoff.

Record the inspected artifact, viewports and interaction states, offending passages and fixes, and any inspection limits. Zero remaining seller-facing passages outside prescribed image placeholders is a mandatory acceptance condition. Fix accepted violations and recheck affected rendering/states before delivery; an uninspected surface cannot be marked passed. For copy-only work inspect the supplied scope and disclose that no rendered-page sweep occurred. Reapply this sweep to the implemented page before launch.

## Findings and remediation

For each material finding return: exact passage or artifact location; likely buyer objection/friction; reasoning grounded in the supplied facts and copy principles; smallest useful correction; and any evidence needed. Classify it as a concrete defect, a supported editorial opportunity, or a hypothesis requiring research/testing. Prioritize consequential findings; avoid quotas and vague requests to make the page more compelling.

The writer evaluates every finding and records acceptance or rejection with reasons. Fix concrete defects and accepted editorial opportunities within the authorized design scope. Re-render and recheck affected copy, controls, layout and image specifications; obtain focused independent verification of consequential fixes. One coordinated round is the default, not a chain of full redesign reviews. Further review is warranted only for unresolved material issues or newly introduced defects.

Do not deliver the wireframe as reviewed/ready while an accepted, locally fixable finding remains unresolved. If a correction requires missing merchant evidence, remove the unsupported claim or qualify it accurately where possible. Only when a material claim or purchase detail must remain in the proposed published copy should the specific evidence gap and launch consequence appear in the separate handoff. Ask only for facts or decisions that materially block progress. Do not fabricate reassurance.

Include a concise review record with the wireframe: independent participation, important findings, accepted fixes and verification, rejected findings with reasons, and any material remaining evidence gaps or useful test hypotheses. Editorial judgment is not proven conversion uplift. User approval, final-image approval and deployment authorization remain separate; this gate grants none of them.

If independent-agent capability is unavailable, perform a clearly labeled separate editorial pass using the same brief, disclose that independence was unavailable, and never claim an independent review occurred. Lack of that capability does not justify skipping review or remediation.
